# Locally hosted typefaces

The Latin subsets of these typefaces are hosted locally to keep the experience independent of third-party font requests.

- **DM Sans**, Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans
- **IBM Plex Mono**, IBM: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono
- **Italiana**, Santiago Orozco: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Italiana

Each is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The original license notices are included alongside the font files.
