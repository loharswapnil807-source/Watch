1541GENEVA, SWITZERLAND

A different kind
of precious.

As Geneva's goldsmiths turned toward watchmaking, a new form of artistry emerged. Ornament gave way to ingenuity. Beauty found its purpose in precision.

By 1601, Geneva had its own watchmakers' guild. In the generations that followed, the craft travelled into the Jura mountains.

03 — A DIFFERENT KIND OF LEGACYBENGALURU, INDIA · 1961
IN

Precision found
a new purpose.

In India, the watch became more than a measure of time. It became a symbol of possibility.

In 1961, HMT partnered with Citizen of Japan to bring watch manufacturing to Bengaluru. Mechanical timekeeping became part of everyday life: a first salary, a graduation, a gift passed from one generation to the next.
